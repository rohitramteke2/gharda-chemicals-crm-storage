NSSM site is down. No worries — let's use Task Scheduler instead, works just as well:

powershell
$action = New-ScheduledTaskAction -Execute "C:\Program Files\nodejs\node.exe" -Argument "server.js" -WorkingDirectory "C:\Users\zoho.crm\Desktop\production\gharda-crm-api"
$trigger = New-ScheduledTaskTrigger -AtStartup
$settings = New-ScheduledTaskSettingsSet -AllowStartIfOnBatteries -DontStopIfGoingOnBatteries -StartWhenAvailable -RestartCount 3 -RestartInterval (New-TimeSpan -Minutes 1)
Register-ScheduledTask -TaskName "ZohoAPI" -Action $action -Trigger $trigger -Settings $settings -User "zoho.crm" -RunLevel Highest -Description "Zoho CRM API Server"
